## Field Notes

Different work environments require different configurations, and this is where Chinese manufacturers can provide strong value. For a city sanitation department, a sewer jetting truck should be configured for daily blockage removal, routine pipeline flushing, and emergency callouts. In this case, buyers usually choose a 6,000 to 10,000 liter water tank, corrosion-resistant piping, and a durable hose reel with 80 to 120 meters of high-pressure hose.

For overseas buyers, the key is matching the truck specification to the actual cleaning distance and pipe diameter. A compact unit works well for urban lanes and residential districts. A larger truck is better for main sewer lines, industrial parks, and highway drainage. If the fleet serves multiple regions, it is also important to confirm whether a LHD sewer jetting truck maintenance plan is available locally, because service accessibility directly affects uptime.
