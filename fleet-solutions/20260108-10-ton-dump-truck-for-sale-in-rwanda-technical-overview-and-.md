## Field Notes

The biggest mistake overseas buyers make is comparing only payload and forgetting duty cycle. A dump truck used for gravel on short routes is very different from a tanker operating on municipal water delivery, or a mixer working in stop-and-go urban pouring. The chassis, PTO setup, suspension, and braking system affect uptime more than brochure figures.

One frequent issue is the small hardware buyers ignore, such as the dump truck door latch. A weak latch, poor cab sealing, or low-grade hinge set can create repeated damage on rough roads and become a safety problem. Another common error is choosing an engine that is too small for the load. Underpowered trucks consume more fuel per ton moved because they spend longer in low gears. Over a 3-year cycle, fuel, tires, hydraulic repairs, and downtime can add 20% to 40% above the purchase price.
