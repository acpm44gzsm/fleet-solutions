## FAQ

**Q: which is better broom or vacuum**

For special trucks, a broom sweeper is generally better for heavy debris, construction dust, gravel, and outdoor road cleaning. It is more durable, simpler to maintain, and performs well on rough surfaces. A vacuum sweeper is better for finer dust control, urban streets, and areas where cleaner operation and less dust emission are important. If your work involves mixed waste or larger particles, choose a broom system. If you need high dust suppression and cleaner results, choose a vacuum system. The best option depends on the application, surface condition, and dust control requirements.

**Q: how to buy a tipper truck**

To buy a tipper truck, first define your application: payload, terrain, axle configuration, and dump body size. Then choose between new or used units based on your budget and operating hours. Check key specifications such as engine power, transmission, hydraulic system, chassis strength, and compliance with local regulations. Request a full inspection report, service history, and photos or videos if buying remotely. Compare quotations from multiple suppliers, including shipping, taxes, and after-sales support. If you need financing, prepare company documents and proof of business use. A new tipper truck typically ranges from about USD 60,000 to USD 180,000+, depending on size and configuration.

**Q: why are square body trucks so expensive**

Square body trucks are often expensive because demand has grown faster than supply. These trucks are now considered classics, especially well-preserved or original examples, so collectors, restorers, and enthusiasts compete for them. Prices also rise when the truck is rust-free, has low mileage, matches original specs, or has desirable trim and drivetrain options. Restoration costs can be high as well, since finding quality parts and labor for older trucks is often expensive. In short, the market price reflects rarity, condition, and strong buyer interest rather than age alone.
