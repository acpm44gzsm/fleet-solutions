## FAQ

**Q: can you finance a box truck**

Generally yes, with one condition: buy from a real manufacturer, not the lowest quotation. A properly specced box truck handles commercial duty cycles well; the failures you hear about usually trace to overloaded cheap builds with no parts support. Ask for the factory license, shipment history and a written 12-month warranty, and use third-party inspection before the balance payment.

**Q: can you buy an mrap**

Yes, MRAPs (Mine-Resistant Ambush Protected vehicles) can be purchased in some cases, but availability depends on local laws, export controls, and the vehicle’s prior status. In the U.S., surplus MRAPs are sometimes released through government auctions or military surplus channels, usually to qualified buyers such as defense contractors, law enforcement agencies, or approved collectors. Civilian access is highly restricted in many countries, and export may require special permits. If you are considering one for security, training, or display purposes, expect significant compliance, transport, and maintenance costs. Typical prices can range from about USD 20,000 to USD 250,000+, depending on condition, configuration, and paperwork.
