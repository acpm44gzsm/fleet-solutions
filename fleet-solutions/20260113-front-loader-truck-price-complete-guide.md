**Q: how bitumen is produced**

Bitumen is produced as a heavy residual product during crude oil refining. After crude oil is heated and separated in the distillation process, the lighter fractions such as gasoline, diesel, and kerosene are removed first. The remaining thick residue is then further processed, often through vacuum distillation or air blowing, to achieve the required viscosity, penetration, and softening point for road or industrial use. In some cases, bitumen can also be blended or modified with polymers to improve performance. It is commonly used in asphalt paving, roofing, waterproofing, and industrial sealing applications.
