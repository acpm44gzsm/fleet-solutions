For buyers importing a transit mixer height from China, the recommended process is: (1) obtain formal quotations from at least three verified manufacturers, (2) arrange third-party pre-shipment inspection (SGS, BV, or TUV), (3) use 30% T/T deposit with balance against shipping documents, and (4) confirm written warranty terms including claim response times. **Q: what is rollback tow truck**

A rollback tow truck is a purpose-built towing and recovery vehicle mounted on a truck chassis, engineered for daily commercial duty rather than general haulage. Chinese export versions typically use Dongfeng, HOWO, FAW or ISUZU chassis with a specialized body, and are supplied with FOB pricing, CE or destination-market certificates and 12-month warranties. Most units ship by RO-RO or flat-rack container with lead times of 30-60 days.
