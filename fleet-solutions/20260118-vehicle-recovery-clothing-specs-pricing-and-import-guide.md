# Vehicle Recovery Clothing: Specs, Pricing and Import Guide

> 车辆 清障 clothing

The vehicle recovery clothing is typically configured with a power take-off (PTO) system driving the specialized equipment. Engine options range from 180 to 420 hp depending on capacity requirements, with 6-speed to 12-speed manual or AMT gearboxes. Emission standards range from Euro 3 to Euro 6 depending on the destination market's requirements.

When sourcing a vehicle recovery clothing from China, buyers should focus on three key areas: chassis reliability, body construction quality, and after-sales parts availability. Chinese export builds pair proven engines (Cummins, Weichai, ISUZU-derived units) with inexpensive spare parts networks in every major African and Asian port city, making them the dominant choice for fleet operators.

Pricing for a vehicle recovery clothing varies significantly based on chassis origin, body capacity, and specification level. Chinese chassis builds offer the best value, while Japanese (ISUZU, Hino) and European (MAN, Volvo, Scania) options command premiums of 25% to 65%. Ocean freight by RO-RO or container adds $3,000 to $8,000 depending on destination port.

Applications for the vehicle recovery clothing span municipal services, construction sites, industrial facilities and agricultural operations. In African markets, government and NGO procurement drives significant demand, while in Southeast Asia the private construction sector is the primary buyer. Latin American buyers increasingly source directly from Chinese factories to bypass import markup layers.
