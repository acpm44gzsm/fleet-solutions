## Field Notes

The biggest mistake is buying for capacity alone. Many overseas buyers ask only for a **1000-3000 gallon high pressure sewer jetting truck** without checking whether their local roads, disposal rules, and crew habits match the configuration. A 3,000-gallon truck may be ideal for a large city in Saudi Arabia or Indonesia, but in older European districts or mountainous Latin American towns, a smaller chassis can be easier to operate and less expensive to maintain.

Operating cost is shaped by fuel use, pump wear, tire load, and crew efficiency. On average, buyers should expect:
- higher fuel consumption on heavy vacuum combinations
- regular replacement of hoses, nozzles, and seals
- pump maintenance tied closely to water quality
- chassis servicing based on local diesel standards and parts access
