## Field Notes

For overseas buyers, the best approach is simple: audit the factory, verify certifications, confirm after-sales support, and compare total landed cost, not just the FOB price. That is how you reduce risk and choose a Chinese special truck supplier that can support long-term business growth.

A serious supplier should also be open to a factory audit by the buyer or a third-party inspection company such as SGS, Bureau Veritas, or TÜV. During the audit, inspect raw materials, weld quality, hydraulic systems, electrical routing, and the final assembly line. If possible, review a completed truck of the same model you intend to buy. That is often more valuable than a brochure.
