## Field Notes

A 6x4 chassis with 220–320 hp is usually enough for plow and spreader work on highways. If the truck must climb grades or run in deep snow, a 4x4 or heavy 6x6 setup can be worth the extra **USD 10,000–30,000** upfront because it reduces towing, downtime, and premature wear. This is where total cost of ownership becomes more important than purchase price.

For overseas buyers importing a **snow removal truck** from China for road maintenance projects, the first mistake is often focusing only on the snowplow or spreader equipment. In real field use, the chassis decides whether the truck can survive cold starts, steep grades, heavy salt loads, and long idle hours. In other words, the chassis brand is not a background detail; it is the foundation of total uptime.
