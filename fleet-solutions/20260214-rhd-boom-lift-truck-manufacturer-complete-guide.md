In practice, many first-time buyers focus too much on lifting height and ignore the engineering details that drive performance and resale value.

Total cost of ownership often decides whether a supplier is truly reliable. A truck that costs USD 8,000 less at purchase can become expensive if it consumes hydraulic oil, needs frequent seal replacement, or waits weeks for a nonstandard control valve. Practical buyers compare not only the invoice price, but also spare part availability, tire and chassis serviceability, fuel consumption, and resale prospects.
